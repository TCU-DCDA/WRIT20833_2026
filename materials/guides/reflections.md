# Self-Reflections
## Three short reflections that build your case

Three reflections, about 300–500 words each, turned in through **TCU Online → Assignments** by the start of class on the due date. Together they're the record you'll draw on in Reflection 3, when you make the case for your final grade.

## Reflection 1 — "Where I'm starting" (due Fri 10/23) {#r1}

This reflection is a starting point, not a test. You'll come back to it in Reflection 2 (halfway) and Reflection 3 (your final self-evaluation), so write it for your future self as much as for me.

Write about each of these:

- **Where you're coming from.** What experience do you have with coding, data, or thinking about the roles computers play in society? If you have none, that's fine.
- **What draws you in, and what worries you.** What are you looking forward to in this course, and what makes you uneasy? Both are useful to know.
- **A prediction you can check later.** Name one thing you think computers *can* tell us about culture, and one thing they *can't*. Please be specific enough that in December you could say whether you were right.
