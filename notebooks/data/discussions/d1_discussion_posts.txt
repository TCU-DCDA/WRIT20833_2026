I was sorted into the category of passing the Excel certification test recently.  This means I am in the category of a "Neeley student" for the time being.  Those who do not pass the Excel test "fail" and if you are in the "fail" category by a certain date, you are dismissed from the Neeley School of Business.  The category includes that I met the minimum requirements, but does not specify by how much or which categories I excelled in.  It also does not account for how many times someone has taken the test in the past.  Anybody taking the test who has at least a 700/1000 passes and acquires the Microsoft certification.  Anyone who has below a 700 at all can not pass the test on that attempt.  Microsoft Incorporation set that threshold as they probably deemed a 700/1000 to be the minimum someone must know to be able to sufficiently perform and manipulate Excel in the classroom or at work.  This is so the test is doing its job and only passing people who can actually use Excel to a decent level.
